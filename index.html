Who we are

A nonprofit devoted to robotics education

Radiant Robotics is a nonprofit organization that brings robotics to students in two ways: engaging STEM education programs, and robust support for competitive robotics teams.

Robotics brings together coding, mechanical design, electronics, and teamwork in a way few other activities can. When students design a robot, test it, and watch it work, they see that they can solve hard problems. That moment of discovery is what we're here for.

Our name reflects that goal. We want to be a source of light, illuminating pathways into STEM for students who might never have imagined themselves as engineers, programmers, or inventors.

What we do

Two ways we bring robotics to more students

Engaging STEM education

Our educational programs introduce the foundations of programming, engineering, and design to students of all ages. From simple circuit building to autonomous robot movement, we make learning fun and tangible.

Competitive excellence

We provide coaching, resources, and logistical support for teams competing in FIRST and other robotics programs, with a focus on teamwork, problem solving, and professional mentorship.
